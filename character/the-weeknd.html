The Verdict

The Weeknd is INFP. Who They Are

Who Is The Weeknd?

Abel Makkonen Tesfaye was born on 16 February 1990 in Toronto, the son of the Ethiopian immigrants Makkonen Tesfaye and Samrawit Hailu, and was raised mainly by his mother and grandmother in Scarborough. At seventeen he left school and moved into an apartment in the Parkdale neighbourhood. The stage name records the exit: he left home on a weekend, and dropped the final e to avoid a trademark conflict with a Canadian band.

He started putting music on YouTube anonymously in August 2009, and in December 2010 uploaded three tracks under the username xoxxxoooxo without attaching a name or a face to any of it. Drake noticed, then Pitchfork, then the New York Times, and for a considerable period nobody knew who he was. The three 2011 mixtapes - House of Balloons in March, Thursday in August and Echoes of Silence in December - established the sound that a great deal of the following decade of R&B was built on. He signed to Republic in September 2012 and collected them as Trilogy, which entered the Billboard 200 at number four.

What are The Weeknd's biggest records?

Beauty Behind the Madness in August 2015 went straight to number one and produced The Hills and Can't Feel My Face. Starboy followed in November 2016 with Daft Punk on the title track, which also topped the chart and has since been certified diamond. Then After Hours arrived on 20 March 2020 carrying Blinding Lights, which became the best-performing song in the entire history of the Billboard Hot 100 and the most-streamed track on Spotify. On 7 February 2021 he headlined the Super Bowl LV halftime show, the first Canadian solo artist to do it, reportedly spending seven million dollars of his own money on the production. He has four Grammys, eight diamond-certified singles, and the highest-grossing tour ever by a male solo artist.

The Journey

The Weeknd: What the INFP Profile Explains

The anonymity is the most INFP thing about his early career, and it was not a marketing device - it lasted longer and cost more than a marketing device would. Dominant Fi produces an artist whose material is uncomfortably personal and who therefore has a strong instinct to keep the person separate from it. He put out three mixtapes about drugs, dissociation and self-disgust and declined to attach a face to any of them, because the songs were the disclosure and anything further was surplus.

The auxiliary Ne is why the sound was new. He was pulling in Cocteau Twins and Siouxsie and the Banshees samples, ambient textures and eighties synth-pop into an R&B frame that had not previously had room for any of it, and doing so at nineteen. Fi supplies the emotional core, Ne supplies the unexpected material it gets built from, and the combination is why House of Balloons sounded like it came from nowhere.

Why did The Weeknd change his sound so drastically?

Because the tertiary Si eventually turns backward, and After Hours is the record where that happens. The eighties synth-pop of Blinding Lights is not a trend chase; it is a deliberate return to a specific remembered sonic world, deployed with real affection and real precision. INFPs reach for Si in that way - as nostalgia with an emotional charge attached rather than as pastiche. The inferior Te is visible in the opposite direction: the business scale of what he now runs, the tour records, the seven million dollars of his own money spent on a halftime show, all of it managed in a way that would have been unimaginable for the person who would not do an interview in 2011.

Letter by Letter

The Weeknd: INFP Letter by Letter

IIntroverted

He built an entire early career without appearing in public, refusing interviews and performing rarely, and even now the persona is a construction held at some distance from the person. The energy runs inward; the enormous public apparatus is something he assembled around himself rather than something he sought.

NIntuitive

The music is atmospheric and associative rather than literal. He builds records as extended worlds with recurring imagery and a narrative running underneath, and his sonic references come from places nobody expected an R&B record to reach for.

FFeeling

The material is confessional to a degree that has often worked against him commercially, and the emotional standard it is measured against is entirely internal. He has never written to be liked, which is why the early mixtapes still sound like documents rather than product.

PPerceiving

His output is exploratory and keeps changing shape: free mixtapes, then a major label, then a hard turn into eighties synth-pop, then another turn. Each era is an opening rather than a consolidation, and he has never settled into the formula that any of them made available.

Why Not Another Type?

Why The Weeknd Is INFP, Not ISFP or INFJ

ISFP is the most common alternative, given the aesthetic control and the sensuality of the material. The distinction is Se against Ne. An Fi-Se artist works with what is physically present, and their imagery is immediate and tactile. Tesfaye's writing is associative and atmospheric - the imagery is dreamlike, the settings are half-real, and the records are built as concept pieces with running narratives across them rather than as documents of moments.

INFJ comes up because the albums are conceptually structured and the persona is carefully managed. But INFJ leads with Ni and pairs it with Fe, which orients the work toward an audience and its response. His material is conspicuously not calibrated to how it will be received - it is confessional to the point of being unflattering, and he has repeatedly released things that complicated his own commercial position. Fi first.

About INFP

The INFP Personality

Values-driven and deeply internal. INFPs hold firm ethical convictions that are entirely their own and rarely up for debate. They are not passive - their gentleness conceals a strong will about what matters.
