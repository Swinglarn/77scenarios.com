The Verdict

Richard Rankin is INFP. The Scottish actor plays Roger MacKenzie across seasons two through eight of Outlander. Outside acting he is a serious amateur photographer - a solitary, observational pursuit that fits the INFP's relationship to the world. He is thoughtful and quietly spoken in interviews, engaged with the emotional interior of his characters rather than their surface presentation.

Who They Are

Richard Rankin and the INFP Mind

Richard Rankin is INFP. The type is defined by a hierarchy of cognitive functions - not a checklist of traits but an ordered stack of mental processes that determines how someone perceives the world and how they make decisions. For Richard Rankin, that stack leads with Fi (Introverted Feeling) and is supported by Ne (Extraverted Intuition). Understanding that order explains not just what Richard Rankin does but why - and why they respond the way they do under pressure, in relationships, and at the turning points of their arc.

Richard Rankin's inner life is the primary reality. Fi (Introverted Feeling) is the dominant function - a deeply personal value system that Richard Rankin uses to evaluate everything, quietly and constantly. It is not visible on the surface, which is why Richard Rankin is often misread as passive or vague: the intensity is entirely internal. Ne (Extraverted Intuition) as the auxiliary generates the possibilities, connections, and alternative framings that allow Richard Rankin to imagine how things could be rather than just how they are. The inferior Te (Extraverted Thinking) is the consistent weak point: execution, structure, external accountability, and the translation of the interior vision into concrete outcomes are where Richard Rankin loses ground and where growth, when it comes, is most visible.

What makes Richard Rankin a compelling example of INFP is not that they demonstrate every item on the type description, but that the function stack holds under pressure. In the moments that define their story in Actor (Outlander, Burnistoun) - the decisions under stress, the failures of judgment, the rare moments of genuine growth - the pattern is consistent. The dominant function is what Richard Rankin reaches for first. The inferior function is what catches them off guard.

The Journey

Richard Rankin: What the INFP Profile Explains

Richard Rankin did not go straight from school to drama school. He studied engineering at university and worked as an engineer before following acting, which he had been drawn to since childhood. The INFP's most characteristic vocational story is the one where the institutional path and the interior path diverge early, and the interior path eventually produces enough pressure that the institutional path is abandoned. He spent years as an engineer while the interior assessment of where the vocation lay ran in parallel and eventually prevailed. He trained, found work in Scottish television and theatre, built a career slowly, and arrived at Roger MacKenzie in Outlander in his mid-thirties after a decade of professional acting.

The Roger MacKenzie arc across six seasons of Outlander is an INFP writing the INFP's most characteristic story without being aware of it: the historian who ends up inhabiting the history he studied, the singer who finds his vocation in an eighteenth-century community that needs exactly what he has spent his life preparing to offer, the man whose values are tested by damage that should have broken them and do not. Rankin plays this with a quality of interior truth that is difficult to fake - the specific texture of a person whose most important convictions are not available for strategic revision, whose most significant processing happens in private and surfaces only when the situation extracts it. The type plays the type, and the result is the most emotionally coherent character arc in the series.

His photography is the public window into what the INFP does between professional demands. He is a serious amateur photographer - the camera pointed at landscapes, at the specific physical reality of the Scottish Highlands and wherever else the work takes him. This is not the photography of someone building a social media presence. It is the documentation of what the interior finds significant in the passing present, which is the Introvert's most natural relationship to the visual world.

Why Not Another Type?

Why Richard Rankin Is INFP, Not ISFP or INFJ

Rankin is sometimes typed ENFP because of the warmth he projects in press appearances and the range of interests that emerge in interviews. The ENFP case rests on Ne dominance: the external world is the generator, and the interior responds to what the world produces. Rankin's direction runs the other way. The interior assessment is primary. The photography, the engineering, the acting - these are domains the Fi designated as mattering before the exterior world offered them as opportunities. He is not following what the world offers. He is following what the interior has assessed as the correct direction and finding the world's offerings that serve that assessment. That inside-out direction is INFP.

INFJ is argued because of the apparent private depth and the sense of a man operating from an interior vision. The distinction is the function that leads: INFJ's Ni produces a comprehensive synthesising framework about where things are heading and what they mean. Rankin does not give interviews that suggest this kind of synthetic vision. He gives interviews about specific experiences - the specific role, the specific preparation, the specific thing that happened on the specific day that made the specific scene work. The orientation is toward the specific and the accumulated rather than the pattern and the synthesised. That specific-and-accumulated orientation is Si as the auxiliary, confirming INFP rather than INFJ.

The engineering background is the most reliable type evidence. The ENFP with an engineering background uses it as one interesting domain among many. The INFJ with an engineering background synthesises it into a framework about how systems work. Rankin used it as a livelihood for years while the Fi worked out what the vocation actually required, then left it when the interior assessment produced a clear enough verdict. That relationship to the engineering - instrumental to the interior's process, not primary in itself - is the INFP's most honest vocational structure.
